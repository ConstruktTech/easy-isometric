# What's new

Updates to Easy Isometric for iPad and iPhone, plus changes to the website. App Store releases, internal previews, and work in development have separate availability labels.

## 0.26 preview

> **Internal TestFlight.** Updated October 31, 2026. This version is not released on the App Store.

### Improvements

- Drawing saves show Saving, Saved, or Save failed. Retry a failed save or recover the last valid copy of a drawing.
- Sync checks that each project contains all its sheets before upload. Incomplete projects cannot replace a complete cloud copy.
- PDF export offers Letter, A4, and Tabloid paper sizes. Dense drawings use numbered detail pages and compact labels. Bill of materials pages use the selected paper size.
- Subscription purchases first check unfinished Apple transactions. This helps a new purchase proceed after an expired renewal.

### Fixes

- Sync & Log Out keeps you signed in and retains local drawings when sync fails. You can retry after the connection recovers.

## Website refresh

> **Available now.** October 22, 2026. No app update is required.

- Refreshed the website while keeping existing tutorial and policy links available.
- Kept the drawing tutorials and product information together on the home page.
- Updated the contact form and clarified the 48-hour app trial and Apple's separate introductory subscription offer.

## 0.25

> **Released on the App Store.** October 22, 2026. Requires iOS 15 or later.

### Improvements

- Improved subscription purchases and Restore Purchases, including recovery of eligible older purchases.
- Improved sign-in recovery and access after an offline launch.
- Improved saved drawing handling when updating the app.
- Added an account deletion request in Profile.
- Clarified trial access and subscription messages.

### Fixes

- Fixed pipe length editing at whole-foot values.
- Corrected nominal pipe size labels in the bill of materials and PDF output while preserving metric settings.

## In development

> **Under test.** These sync changes are not enabled in the customer app. No public release date is set.

- Keep separate copies when two devices change the same drawing.
- Retain pending edits through connection failures, app restarts, and account switching.
- Add drawing history, Trash, and Restore controls for sync recovery.
